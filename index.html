Machine Learning Scientist @ Applied ML Group, Fraunhofer HHI

Researcher and engineer interested in data-centric and user-centric machine learning. I hold an MSc in Cognitive Systems and have a background in mathematics and computer science. I currently post-train large language models for multimodal agentic tasks.

Before LLMs, I worked across computer vision, speech, and computational linguistics — vision-language and self-supervised models for chest X-rays, production speech-recognition systems for LabTwin’s life-sciences voice assistant, and argumentation mining and climate change discourse at the Applied CL Discourse Research Lab.

My broader background spans applied research, large-scale model training on HPC clusters, uncertainty quantification, and reliable machine learning systems.
